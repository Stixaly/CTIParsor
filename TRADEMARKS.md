# CTIParsor name and logo

This policy concerns the **CTIParsor** project name and the project logo at
[`frontend/public/logo.png`](frontend/public/logo.png). The official project
repository is [Stixaly/CTIParsor](https://github.com/Stixaly/CTIParsor).
It does not cover the names, logos or icons of third parties bundled with
the project. This policy does not assert that a mark is registered.

## Software rights and branding

The [AGPL](LICENSE), explained in [NOTICE](NOTICE), grants
rights to use, modify and redistribute the software, including commercially.
Those rights do not grant trademark rights to present a product or service
as official, endorsed or operated by the CTIParsor project.

This policy reserves those branding uses to the extent protected by
applicable trademark law. It does not change the copyright licence of the
logo image, prohibit lawful descriptive use, or impose a fee or approval
requirement for commercial use of the software.

## References that do not need permission

You may truthfully identify CTIParsor in attribution, documentation,
comparisons, reviews, discussions and screenshots, without suggesting an
endorsement. For example, an independent product may say "Based on
CTIParsor" and link to the original project. Preserving the attribution in NOTICE
is permitted and does not imply an affiliation.

You may accurately describe an unmodified redistribution as CTIParsor,
provided its presentation identifies the distributor and does not imply
that the project operates or endorses the distribution. There is no blanket
requirement to rename every copy. Other uses permitted by applicable law
remain permitted.

## Independent products and services

Clearly identify your own organisation or project as the provider of an
independent fork, distribution or hosted service. Do not present it as an
official CTIParsor release or service, or suggest the project's endorsement
without permission. Use distinct branding where use of the CTIParsor name
or logo would otherwise create confusion about origin or affiliation.

For example, "Example Service — based on CTIParsor; independently operated
by Example" distinguishes the provider from the upstream project. An
"Official CTIParsor Cloud" claim would require authorisation.

To request permission for official branding or endorsement, contact the
maintainers through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Stixaly/CTIParsor/issues).
Permission must come from a person authorised to grant the relevant rights;
an unanswered request is not permission. This contact route is for branding
requests, not a prerequisite for using or selling AGPL-compliant software.
